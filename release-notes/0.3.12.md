## Before you update

**Offline batteries now show `unavailable` instead of `unknown`.** This covers a battery's charge, power, solar and PV
sensors, and the battery power and stored energy totals when no battery can be read. Automations that test these for
`unknown` need to test for `unavailable`.

**Each battery now has its own device** (*HomePower <name>*). Entity IDs and names stay as they were. If you had put the
HomePower device in an area, give the battery devices that area too, and check any automation or dashboard that picks
entities by the HomePower device.

**Anker X1 solar was under-counted.** HomePower totalled a figure that reads about 100 W below what the PV inputs
produce, so little or nothing was counted in weak light. It now uses the total of the PV inputs. Solar already recorded
stays as it was.

After updating, check that your HomePower sensors, icons and automations behave as you expect.
